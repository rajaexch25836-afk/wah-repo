    Dashboard
    View store

    Drag ⠿ (or use ↑ ↓) to set the order products show in on the store (“Featured”).

    Collections

    Group products like “Eid Collection” or “Winter Sale”. Each collection gets a button on the store, and can have its own row on the home page.

    Staff logins

    Give your team their own login with only the parts they need — e.g. a packer who sees orders but cannot change prices. Staff log in on this page with their username and password. Only you (the owner) can manage staff, security and backups.

    Customer accounts

    Payment methods

    Bank / Easypaisa / JazzCash accounts

    Customers see these accounts at checkout, send the money, and enter the transaction ID (TID). Check the payment and use “Mark as paid” on the order.

    Delivery charges

    Added to the order total at checkout. Set 0 for free delivery.

    Different charge for some cities e.g. your own city cheaper

    Home page slider

    Up to 6 pictures that slide at the top of the store. Customers can swipe them on phones. Changes here save straight away.

    Store details

    Social media & contact

    Leave a field empty to hide it on the website.

    WhatsApp alerts

    Get a WhatsApp message on your phone for every new order, free, through CallMeBot. One-time setup:

    1. Save +34 684 78 33 47 in your phone contacts (name it CallMeBot).
    2. On WhatsApp send it: I allow callmebot to send me messages
    3. It replies with your API key (if not within 2 minutes, try again after 24 hours). Enter it below, save, and press “Send test message”.

    CallMeBot is a free outside service for personal use: messages can sometimes be late, and order details (customer name, phone, address) pass through it. Every order is always saved in the Orders tab either way.
